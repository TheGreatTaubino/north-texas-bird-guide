## Full Description
*(4000 char limit)*

```
A field guide built for birders in the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex, Plano, and Collin County.

SPECIES COVERAGE
109 birds organized by group — Songbirds, Birds of Prey, Ducks, Geese, Gulls, Shorebirds, and Waterbirds. Each species includes:
• Photo identification
• Key field marks (size, color, shape)
• In-flight identification tips
• North Texas habitat and behavior notes
• Fun facts and trivia

SIGHTING TRACKER
Log every bird you spot. The built-in calendar shows your sighting history by month so you can track what you're finding and when. Backup and restore your data at any time, or sync across devices using a private GitHub Gist.

LOCAL BIRDING SPOTS
Curated list of the best DFW birding locations — Arbor Hills Nature Preserve, Trinity River Audubon Center, Heard Natural Science Museum, and more — matched to the species most likely found there.

TRIVIA
Test your knowledge with species trivia covering behavior, biology, and local ecology.

FILTERS & SEARCH
Filter by season (year-round, summer, winter, migrant), by group, or search by common or scientific name. Quick-ID mode highlights the most frequently seen birds.

WORKS OFFLINE
No internet connection required. The full guide — photos included — is available the moment you open the app. Great for use in the field.

NO ADS. NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED.
Your sighting data stays on your device. No sign-up, no tracking, no ads.

Built for North Texas birders by a North Texas birder.
```

## Screenshots Required

Google Play requires **at least 2 phone screenshots**. Recommended: 4–5.

Capture these screens on a phone (or in Chrome DevTools mobile simulator at 390×844):

| # | Screen to Capture | Why |
|---|------------------|-----|
| 1 | Main bird grid — scroll to show 6–8 cards with photos | First impression |
| 2 | A single expanded bird card showing photo + field marks | Shows depth of content |
| 3 | Filter bar open — season chips visible | Shows filtering |
| 4 | Sighting Calendar month view with some birds logged | Shows tracker feature |
| 5 | Birding Spots section | Shows local value |

**Screenshot specs:** 16:9 or 9:16, minimum 320px on short side, maximum 3840px on long side. PNG or JPEG.

**How to take them in Chrome:**
1. Open `dist/index.html` in Chrome
2. DevTools (F12) → Toggle device toolbar → select iPhone 12 Pro or Pixel 5
3. Screenshot via DevTools camera icon (top-right of device frame)
