Privacy Policy

North Texas Bird Guide  ·  Last updated: May 24, 2026

Overview

North Texas Bird Guide is a local field guide app for birds in the Dallas–Fort Worth area. It does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your sighting log locally on your device using the browser's localStorage API. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to sync it (see below). You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings.

Optional GitHub Sync

The app includes an optional feature to back up and sync your sighting data to a private GitHub Gist of your choosing. This feature requires you to provide a GitHub personal access token with gist scope only.

  • Your token is stored in sessionStorage — it is cleared automatically when you close the app.
  • Your Gist ID is stored in localStorage so the sync form can pre-fill on your next session.
  • No token or sighting data is sent to any server operated by the developer.
  • Data is sent directly from your device to GitHub's API (api.github.com) under your own GitHub account.

No Tracking or Analytics

The app contains no analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting, or any third-party tracking. No data is collected about how you use the app.

No Account Required

You do not need to create an account to use this app. No registration, email address, or personal information is ever requested.

Children

This app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Because no personal data is collected from any user, the app is safe for all ages.
